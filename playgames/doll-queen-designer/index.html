Overview

The activity of dressing up dolls is one of the main reasons dolls exist in the first place and that's why it's been turned into games. This is one of those games that presents the act of dressing up as a runner experience. If only it were truly as easy as running into the clothes that you want to wear to put them on but we don't have such luxury. We can escape into such a fantasy with this game and deal with the obstacles that come as the price for convenience. Remember that even when you're running, there are still opportunities to put an outfit together and look stylish.

Gameplay and Experience

Doll Queen Designer is a 3D dress-up runner game that can be played with just the mouse. To start playing, you need to drag a slider across the screen. You can do this by moving the cursor over to it, clicking, holding the Left Mouse Button, and then dragging it across the screen. You do this to move the running doll back and forth across the lane as they run along a straight track. The goal is to collect the clothes needed to complete an outfit and reach the finish line. Each level presents a different outfit to complete which will appear in the top right corner of the screen. Outfits are made of a top, a bottom, and shoes. Different types will appear along the track and you can collect them by directing the doll into them. While running, you may encounter scissors cutting across the track. If you run into them, they'll remove one article of clothing. When you reach the finish line, you'll receive a score based on how closely the doll's outfit resembles the outfit in the corner.

Tips and Tricks

  • This is pretty casual for a running game with the doll moving at a slow pace and the scissors moving slowly as well.
  • It's more about timing then than about speed and even if you make a mistake, you'll have other opportunities to collect the same clothes.
  • The longer you run, the more cautious you should be.
