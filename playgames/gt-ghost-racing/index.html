Overview

Choose your vehicle and race through the various tracks compete against the ghosts of racing legends to prove you are the best of the best and collect all the stars! Race through the seasons, night tracks, winter tracks and futuristic neon tracks. 8 Cars, 9 Tracks, 27 Stars. Race during the seasons, at night and even an icy track.

Gameplay and Experience

Pick a car, then race! Use arrow keys or WASD for steering, speeding, and stopping. Collect stars and race ahead of ghosts. Master corner drifting to stay competitive. Hint: Slow down slightly on icy courses to maintain control.
