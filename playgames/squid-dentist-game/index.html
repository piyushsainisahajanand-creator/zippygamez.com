Gameplay and Experience

Follow the on-screen objective, use simple controls, and improve through short repeatable rounds.

Controls

  • Clear all tiles to win!OBSERVE: Rotate the 3D structure to inspect stacked layers. Note: only tiles that are not covered by others can be selected.SELECT & COLLECT: Tap an eligible tile. It will drop and be added to the tray at the screen.MATCH 3 & CLEAR: When three identical tiles are in the tray, they automatically merge and vanish, freeing up space.But your tray holds only 7 slots. Use Undo to revert moves, Retrieve up 3 tiles, or Shuffle to rearrange the board and find new match.
