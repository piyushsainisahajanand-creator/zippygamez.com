Privacy Policy

How ZippyGamez handles privacy and data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use ZippyGamez, how cookies and analytics may work, and how third-party advertising and external links are handled.

1. Overview

ZippyGamez is an independent website for app information, playable browser games, store game details, articles, guides, and entertainment-related content. ZippyGamez.com is operated by ZippyGamez PTE. LTD..

We do not require account registration for general browsing or browser-game access.

2. Information We May Collect

Automatically collected information

When you visit the website, standard technical information may be processed by our website systems, hosting providers, analytics tools, browser APIs, or advertising partners. This may include:

  • IP address, approximate location, browser type, device type, operating system, and language settings.
  • Pages visited, time spent, referring pages, clicks, error logs, and performance information.
  • Cookie identifiers or similar identifiers where cookies or advertising technologies are enabled.

Information you provide

If you contact us or use a contact form, we may process the name, email address, subject, message, page URL, and other details you choose to provide so we can respond to your inquiry.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, and similar technologies may be used to keep the website working, measure traffic, understand content performance, improve layout quality, prevent abuse, and support advertising.

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work correctly if cookies or storage are blocked.

4. Analytics

The website may use analytics services such as Google Analytics or similar measurement tools. These services can collect usage and device information through cookies or tags to help us understand page performance, user flow, and technical issues.

Analytics information is used to improve site speed, layout, navigation, and content quality.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Partners

ZippyGamez may display advertising or use advertising-related technologies from third-party partners. These may include Google advertising services such as Google Ads, Google AdSense, Google Ad Manager, Google Ad Exchange (AdX), and other advertising technologies where enabled.

Advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show, measure, limit, and improve ads. They may process browser/device information, page views, ad interactions, and general usage data according to their own policies.

Traffic from platforms such as Meta/Facebook or other advertising networks may also include referral or campaign information that helps measure website visits and performance.

We do not control the data practices of third-party advertising networks. Users should review the privacy choices and policies provided by those partners.

6. Third-Party Links

ZippyGamez may link to Google Play, the Apple App Store, developer websites, third-party game providers, articles, or other external websites. External sites have their own privacy policies, cookies, security practices, and terms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

7. How We Use Information

  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • To understand site traffic, content performance, and user experience.
  • To display and measure advertising where advertising technologies are enabled.
  • To respond to messages, support requests, or correction requests.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, or technical misuse of the website.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information directly to users or unrelated parties. We may share or allow processing of limited information with service providers, analytics providers, advertising partners, hosting providers, security tools, or legal authorities where required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

ZippyGamez is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at contact@zippygamez.com.

10. Your Choices

  • You can manage cookies and storage from your browser settings.
  • You can use third-party ad settings and privacy tools offered by advertising partners.
  • You may contact us to request correction, deletion, or review of information you provided directly to us.

11. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, legal requirements, or partner technologies change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

For privacy questions, contact: contact@zippygamez.com
